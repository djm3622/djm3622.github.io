I attended the **8th Annual Learning for Dynamics & Control Conference (L4DC 2026)** at the University of Southern California, where I presented our work, [**Can Optimal Transport Improve Federated Inverse Reinforcement Learning?**](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v331/millard26a.html). The paper introduces an optimal transport-based approach for combining locally learned reward functions across heterogeneous agents.
