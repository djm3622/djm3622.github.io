I attended the **2026 Joint Mathematics Meetings** in Washington, D.C., where I gave a talk in the **SIAM Minisymposium on Recent Advances in Numerical Linear Algebra**. I presented our work, [**PEARL: Preconditioner Enhancement through Actor-critic Reinforcement Learning**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.10750), which develops a reinforcement learning approach for learning matrix preconditioners.
