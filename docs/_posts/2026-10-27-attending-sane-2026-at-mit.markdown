I'm excited to be attending **SANE 2026 (Speech and Audio in the Northeast)** at **MIT** in Cambridge, Massachusetts on October 30, where I'll be presenting our poster, [**Discrete Parameter Estimation for DDSP Source Separation**]({{ '/assets/presentations/sane2026_poster.pdf' | relative_url }}), with Andrea Cogliati. The work looks at how to fit the integer parameters of a Karplus–Strong string model, its loop delay and pluck position, where gradient descent breaks down, and compares classical search and pitch estimation with relaxed and learned categorical estimators (REINFORCE, Gumbel-Softmax) on mixtures of up to seven strings. [SANE](https://www.saneworkshop.org/sane2026/) is a one-day workshop that brings together speech and audio researchers from across the Northeast.
